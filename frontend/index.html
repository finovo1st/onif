Straight Answers

Everything you need to know about how Finovo manages pooled funds, generates systematic AI income, and distributes weekly returns to your account.

Finovo is an AI-based automatic systematic income generation plan company. We pool capital from investors and deploy it across proprietary algorithmic models and global markets. Investors earn structured weekly returns (ROI) based on their chosen plan up to a 300.00% maximum total return cap.
Our systematic AI models operate autonomously across global markets and digital assets 24/7. Employing dynamic statistical modeling, automated arbitrage, and disciplined risk-hedging algorithms, our systems generate consistent income streams regardless of broader market direction.
After completing standard KYC verification, select your desired plan (Starter, Pro, or Elite), transfer the investment amount using your preferred deposit method, and provide your transaction reference details. Once verified by admin, your plan is immediately active.
Weekly ROI earnings are calculated and credited directly to your account balance every week. You can withdraw your spendable balance anytime using your preferred withdrawal method with rapid turnaround and a low flat transaction fee.
